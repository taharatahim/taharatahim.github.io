## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Verified public contact and privacy information
The landing page SHALL provide a route to contact or learn more about the responsible community through the official Shevet Ahim website and discoverable links to the public privacy and data-deletion page and experimental terms of service. Any specific privacy, confidentiality, data-use, response-time, availability, or religious-oversight claims SHALL be included only when verified and approved by the project or organization.

#### Scenario: Visitor seeks an organizational contact route
- **WHEN** a visitor wants more information about the service
- **THEN** the page provides a working route to the official Shevet Ahim website

#### Scenario: Visitor seeks privacy information
- **WHEN** a visitor wants to learn about data use or request deletion
- **THEN** the landing page provides a working link to the dedicated privacy and data-deletion page

#### Scenario: Visitor seeks terms of service
- **WHEN** a visitor wants to understand the service's terms and experimental limitations
- **THEN** the landing page provides a working link to the dedicated terms page

#### Scenario: Unverified privacy or service claim is unavailable
- **WHEN** a privacy or service detail has not been confirmed by the project or organization
- **THEN** the page does not present that detail as a guarantee or fact
